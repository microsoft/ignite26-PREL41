CONTOSO FRESH PRODUCT RECALL STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

Document ID: CF-QA-SOP-004
Version: 3.2
Effective date: 2026-07-01
Owner: Supplier Quality and Food Safety
Classification: Internal workshop content

1. Purpose

This procedure defines how Contoso Fresh investigates a supplier quality
notice, identifies affected inventory and shipments, and provides governed
recall information to authorized users.

2. Recall objectives

The recall team must answer these questions:

1. Which stores currently hold the affected lot?
2. Which historical lots are related to the supplier batch window?
3. Which trucks carried the affected lots?
4. Did any affected shipment exceed the cold-chain threshold?
5. Is the supplier certification current?
6. Which users are authorized to view each result?

3. Roles

Incident lead
Coordinates the investigation and approves status updates.

Data and analytics engineer
Unifies the required data, validates joins, and publishes the recall console.

Supplier quality lead
Reviews supplier notices, certificates, and corrective actions.

Regional manager
Reviews affected inventory and shipments for an assigned region.

Logistics partner
Reviews only the partner's shipments and cannot view supplier cost data.

4. Required data sources

- Operational orders, shipments, and store inventory.
- Five years of shipment and lot history.
- Truck temperature telemetry.
- Supplier quality certificates and recall notices.
- User and role assignments for governed access.

5. Investigation procedure

1. Record the supplier ID, product, lot, and production window from the notice.
2. Place the affected lot on hold in the operational process.
3. Query current inventory to identify stores holding the affected lot.
4. Trace shipments and trucks associated with the lot.
5. Compare telemetry with the cold-chain threshold.
6. Review the supplier certificate status and expiration date.
7. Identify related lots from the same supplier batch window.
8. Apply regional and partner access restrictions.
9. Publish the validated results in the recall console.
10. Record the final disposition after the supplier investigation is complete.

6. Cold-chain threshold

For this workshop scenario, flag a shipment when recorded temperature remains
above 4 C for more than 20 minutes.

7. Access rules

- Regional managers can view only stores and shipments in their region.
- Logistics partners can view only their shipments.
- Supplier cost data is hidden from logistics partners.
- Supplier quality staff can review certificate and notice documents.

8. Completion criteria

The investigation is ready for management review when the recall console:

- Identifies affected stores and related shipments.
- Shows the temperature status for each affected shipment.
- Links to the supplier notice and certification evidence.
- Returns only the data authorized for the signed-in role.

This is fictional workshop content and is not operational food-safety guidance.
